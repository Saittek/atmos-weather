Solara Weather — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Solara (“the App”) provides weather forecasts, radar, and related tools. This policy explains what data we handle.

Anonymous usage (optional)

In production we may count coarse page views (for example “radar” or “Earth”) to learn which screens are used. We do not store your name, email, exact location, or query strings in these counts. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy analytics off.

Location

If you allow location access, the App uses your approximate or precise location to show local weather, radar, and alerts. Location is sent to weather data providers (for example Open-Meteo, RainViewer tiles, National Weather Service, and Environment Canada) only to fetch forecasts and maps for that place. We do not sell your location data.

Accounts

If you create an account, we store your email, a hashed password, and preferences you choose to save (favorites, units, theme, last place). Passwords are hashed and not stored in plain text.

Device permissions

  • Location — weather for your area (optional; you can search cities instead).
  • Notifications — optional rain and severe weather alerts you enable.

Advertising

The web site may show ads via Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads based on visits to this site and other sites. We do not sell your personal data to advertisers. You can learn more and manage ad personalization via Google Ads Settings and Google’s advertising policies.

The native iOS app shell may omit or limit in-app web ads; this website remains ad-supported unless stated otherwise in the App Store listing.

Third-party services

Weather data is provided by third parties such as Open-Meteo, RainViewer / Iowa Environmental Mesonet, NASA, Ventusky (model maps), the U.S. National Weather Service, and Environment and Climate Change Canada. Their servers may receive the coordinates or city you request. Review their privacy policies for details.

Analytics

We do not run a separate invasive analytics SDK by default. Standard server logs (for example Cloudflare request logs) may retain IP addresses and user-agent strings for security and reliability for a limited time.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. Do not create an account for a child under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions, email yellowknife1989@gmail.com or visit solaraweather.com/support.html.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change when we do.
